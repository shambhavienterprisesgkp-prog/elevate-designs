Sleep dentistry, explained

Three levels of calm — you choose how much.

Every option is administered and monitored by a physician anesthesiologist with 30 years of experience. You're never just “put under” and hoped for the best.

For mild nerves

Oral conscious

A simple pill or liquid takes the edge off. You stay awake and aware, just deeply relaxed and at ease in the chair.

For deeper comfort

IV sedation

A gentle, adjustable level of sedation for longer or more involved treatment. Most patients remember little to nothing.

For complete rest

General anesthesia

Full sleep dentistry in our surgical suite — ideal for complex cases, special needs, or finishing everything in one visit.
