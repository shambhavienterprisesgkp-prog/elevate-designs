Best for · Attics · Rim joists · Walls Expanding foam that seals and insulates in one pass, stopping air leaks other materials miss. Ideal for irregular cavities and the highest R-value per inch — the premium choice for whole-home performance.
Best for · Attics · Existing walls Recycled, dense-packed fibre blown into place to fill gaps completely. A budget-friendly, high-coverage option that's excellent for topping up under-insulated attics in older Nashville homes.
Best for · New builds · Open walls · Floors Pre-cut rolls fitted between studs and joists. The most cost-effective choice for new construction and accessible cavities, with solid thermal and sound performance.
Best for · Sound rooms · Fire safety · Basements Dense rock-based batts that resist fire and dampen sound far better than fiberglass. The go-to for media rooms, shared walls and areas where moisture and fire resistance matter.
Best for · Hot attics · Summer comfort A reflective layer stapled in the attic that bounces radiant heat back out. Pairs with traditional insulation to tame those brutal Tennessee summer attic temperatures and ease AC load.
Best for · Crawl spaces · Moisture control A sealed vapour barrier plus rigid foam that locks out humidity and ground moisture. Stops mould, protects ductwork and makes floors noticeably warmer underfoot.
