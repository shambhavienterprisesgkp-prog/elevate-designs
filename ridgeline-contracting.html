Our Story

Built by hand.
Earned by trust.

Construction has always been in my blood. I grew up building with my father, started flipping homes as a young man, and eventually spent years honing my skills under an experienced general contractor before going out on my own.

I spent time in sales too — and what I learned there was just as valuable: people don't want to be managed. They want to be heard. That's why at Ridgeline, we treat every project as a collaboration. You bring the vision. We bring the craftsmanship and the honesty to get it done right.
