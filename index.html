About us

We're a small business too.

Elevate Designs grew out of a family business that's been running for over 25 years across interiors, furniture and HVAC.

When we needed websites for our own brands — TouchWoods for custom closets & kitchens, Elevate for recliners and sofas, and our HVAC work — we couldn't find anyone who'd make them both beautiful and genuinely useful without charging a fortune or locking us in. So we learned to do it ourselves, and the results worked: real sites, real enquiries, fully in our control.

Now we do the same for other small businesses. It helps that this isn't only an interiors background — our founder worked for years as a business analyst in IT across Canada and the US, gathering requirements and working with development teams to ship real projects, after completing a master's in global management from Arizona, USA. That mix — knowing how to listen to what a business actually needs, and knowing how to get it built — is exactly what goes into every site we make.

Common questions

Got a question?
We've got answers.

How does “design first, pay later” actually work?+

We build a real sample page for your specific business — not a template, a page designed for you — before you spend anything. You watch a personal video walkthrough of exactly how it looks. Then you decide: go live and pay, or walk away and owe us nothing. No invoice, no pressure.

How long does it take?+

Most sites are live in 5–6 days once you share your content. Full turnaround from first contact to live is typically under 2 weeks.

What do I actually own when it’s done?+

Everything. The domain is in your name. The hosting account is yours. The source files are yours. We’re never a middleman — your site keeps running even if you stop working with us.

Do I need to know anything technical?+

Not at all. We handle domain setup, hosting, and going live. You don’t need to touch code or manage any accounts. If you want changes later, just email us.

What if I want changes after the site goes live?+

Just email us any time. Small changes are $39 each, done within 48 hours. Or join the optional Care Plan at $59/mo — unlimited small updates, managed hosting, and priority support.

Will my site work on phones?+

Yes — every site is mobile-first by default. Most of your customers see you on a phone first, so we design for that screen and make sure it’s fast, easy to tap, and looks great.

What if I don’t like the design?+

That’s what the “design first” promise covers. Don’t love it? Walk away — no invoice, no awkward conversation. Like the direction but want tweaks? Every package includes revision rounds before you pay.

Do I need to provide photos and text?+

For the free sample we can start from just a description. For the full site, your own photos and words make a big difference — we’ll guide you on exactly what we need. The Premium package includes copywriting.

I’m not based in the US — can you work with me?+

Yes. We work with small businesses globally. Pricing is in USD, everything is handled remotely — location is no barrier at all.

Do you build online stores?+

Simple product showcases with buy-now links (PayPal, Stripe, Razorpay) are in the Growth package. Full e-commerce with a cart and inventory is a Premium project — reach out and we’ll scope it.
