About us

We're a small business too.

Elevate Designs is part of Shambhavi Enterprises — a family business in Gorakhpur that's been running for over 25 years across interiors, furniture and HVAC.

When we needed websites for our own brands — TouchWoods for modular interiors, Elevate for recliners and sofas, and our HVAC work — we couldn't find anyone who'd make them both beautiful and genuinely useful without charging a fortune or locking us in. So we learned to do it ourselves, and the results worked: real sites, real enquiries, fully in our control.

Now we do the same for other small businesses. It helps that this isn't only an interiors background — our founder worked for years as a business analyst in IT across Canada and the US, gathering requirements and working with development teams to ship real projects, after completing a master's in global management from Arizona, USA. That mix — knowing how to listen to what a business actually needs, and knowing how to get it built — is exactly what goes into every site we make.
