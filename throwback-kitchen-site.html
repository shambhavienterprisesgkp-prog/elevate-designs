Our story

Built For The 90s Kids Who Still Remember The Good Snacks.

Our Mission

Throwback Kitchen exists to bring back the flavors, feelings, and food memories of childhood — reimagined as premium dessert experiences for grown-up taste buds.

Why Now

The kids who grew up with ice cream trucks, lunchbox snacks, candy aisles, Saturday morning cartoons, and after-school treats are adults now. We think they deserve dessert that feels just as fun, but tastes even better.
