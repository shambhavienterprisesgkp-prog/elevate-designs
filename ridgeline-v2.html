Our Story

Built by hand.
Earned by trust.

Construction has always been in my blood. I grew up building alongside my father, started flipping homes as a young man, and spent years honing my craft under an experienced general contractor before starting Ridgeline.

I spent time in sales too — and what I took from it was this: people don't want to be managed. They want to be heard. At Ridgeline, every project is a collaboration. You bring the vision. We bring the craftsmanship and the honesty to build it right.
