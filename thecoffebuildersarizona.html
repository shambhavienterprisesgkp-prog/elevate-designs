The build

Built by makers,
not by a franchise.

Before the first shot was pulled, the founders visited more than 200 coffee shops across the country — studying what worked down to the smallest detail. Then they did what they do best: they built it themselves.

The flowing wood ceiling, the bar, the iron fixtures — all crafted in-house by the team behind Porter Barn Wood and Porter Ironworks. The result is a coffee house that feels handmade because it is.

  • Wood, by handA sculpted timber ceiling that doubles as the showroom's signature.
  • Iron, fabricated in-houseFixtures and details forged by Porter Ironworks.
  • Coffee, obsessed overA menu engineered like everything else here — to be excellent.
